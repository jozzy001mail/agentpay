# Product — AgentPay

AgentPay is a stablecoin payment-link app for small businesses, built for
Kiroween 2026 (Frankenstein category). A market seller creates a payment link,
a customer pays stablecoin into an on-chain escrow, and the seller releases the
funds when the goods are delivered. No account, no KYC, no custody, no fees
from AgentPay.

## Users

- Small-business owners in Nigeria and across Africa who sell on WhatsApp,
  Instagram or in person and need a trustworthy way to take stablecoin
  payments without handing custody to anyone.
- Their customers, who pay with any wallet via a link or QR code.

## Goals

- A merchant can create an invoice and share it in under a minute.
- A customer can pay without an account.
- Money is safe in escrow: only the merchant releases, only the payer gets a
  refund, nobody else can touch it.
- A shop owner who is not technical can drive it by talking to the in-app
  agent in plain English.

## Non-goals

- No mainnet deployment, no real money, no fees.
- No user accounts, no KYC, no price oracle.
- No promises about income, sales or customers.
